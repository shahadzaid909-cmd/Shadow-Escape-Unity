# Shadow Escape — Game Design Summary

**Genre:** 2D action-adventure / survival  
**Mode:** Single-player  
**Setting:** Dark industrial facility  
**Target session length:** Approximately 3–5 minutes per level (design target)

## Core loop
Explore the facility → activate lamps and switches → navigate illuminated safe zones → avoid the shadow creature → reach the exit before time expires.

## Mechanics
- Movement using WASD or arrow keys
- Light-based safety: the shadow creature cannot enter illuminated zones
- Switch interaction to turn lamps on or off
- Countdown timer and three-heart health system
- Win by reaching the exit; lose by exhausting hearts or time

## Level design
1. **Conveyor & Machinery Room:** Belts, crates, barrels, limited visibility, and an illuminated path toward the exit.
2. **Industrial Power Room:** Narrow corridors, electrical panels, and smaller islands of light requiring careful navigation.

## Interface
Main menu, in-game timer and health, pause menu, win screen, and game-over screen.
