# Shadow Escape — Game Design Summary

## Concept

**Shadow Escape** is a single-player, 2D action-adventure survival game in a dark industrial facility. The player navigates dangerous corridors, activates light sources to create safe zones, avoids a shadow creature, and attempts to reach an exit before time expires.

## Game mechanics

- **Movement:** WASD and arrow keys.
- **Lighting:** Switches toggle lamps, revealing paths and creating protected areas.
- **Enemy:** A shadow creature pursues the player in unlit spaces; illuminated areas provide safety.
- **Health:** The player has three hearts.
- **Timer:** The player must escape before the countdown reaches zero.
- **Win/Loss:** Reach the exit to win; lose all hearts or run out of time to lose.

## Level concepts

1. **Conveyor & Machinery Room:** Conveyor belts, machinery, crates, narrow passages, and strategic lighting.
2. **Industrial Power Room:** Dark corridors, electrical panels, obstacles, and isolated safe zones.

## Interface and presentation

The design document describes a main menu, in-game timer and heart indicators, a pause menu, and separate win and loss screens. Visual assets include a player character, shadow enemy, industrial environment tiles, switches, lamps, and lighting effects.

## Implementation and verification

The original project materials include Unity and C# source files. This summary describes the design and reported implementation; it is **not evidence of independent runtime verification**.
